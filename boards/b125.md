## CAN channels: important for the driver

| Channel | Peripheral               | TX            | RX        | AF  | Terminator enable / disable | DE-9 |
| ------- | ------------------------ | ------------- | --------- | --- | --------------------------- | ---- |
| Ch1     | **FDCAN1**               | **PD1** (A10) | PB8 (A2)  | 9   | PC13 (C1) / PE4 (B1)        | J6   |
| Ch2     | **FDCAN3** (not FDCAN2!) | PG9 (A8)      | PG10 (C8) | 2   | PH14 (B12) / PA10 (C14)     | J7   |

- Ch1 TX is on **PD1**, not PB9 as on B135. `examples/h7_embassy` does not run on B125 unchanged.
- Ch2 is **FDCAN3**. This board exercises the FDCAN1 + FDCAN3 instance pair and FDCAN3's share of the message
  RAM. FDCAN2 is not used.
- Transceivers: ADM3050E (isolated CAN FD), one per channel.
- **The two channels are isolated from each other** (`ISO_1` and `ISO_CH2` are separate grounds, each with its
  own isolated 5 V). To connect Ch1 ↔ Ch2, the cable must carry CAN_H, CAN_L **and GND** (pins 3/6).

### Termination (same circuit as B135, per channel)

- Split 2 × 60.4 Ω with a capacitor to the channel's isolated ground, switched by a **latching** relay
  EE2-5TNUH-L (K1 for Ch1, K2 for Ch2). Driven through a TLP2270 opto and BSS138s from the channel's isolated 5 V.
- Pulse `TERM_EN` high for **> 10 ms** to connect the terminator, or `TERM_DIS` for > 10 ms to disconnect it. Keep
  both pins low otherwise and never drive both at once. The state survives resets and power cycles, so HIL tests
  must set it explicitly.
  - These semantics were confirmed on B135. The wiring here is identical (`TERM_EN` → opto → BSS138 → set coil
    on relay pin 12, `TERM_DIS` → reset coil on pin 7, contacts on pins 4/5 and 8/9), so they apply here too.

### DE-9 connectors (J6 = Ch1, J7 = Ch2, male)

- Pin 7 CAN_H, pin 2 CAN_L, pins 3 and 6 isolated GND of that channel, pin 5 shield (0 Ω options to chassis),
  shell = per-channel chassis.
- Pin 9: **bus power input on Ch1 only** (fuse/TVS → LT8304 isolated flyback → +5V0_SYS). On Ch2, pin 9 is not
  connected to anything. TVS assembly variants: `buspower_iso` SMAJ58CA, `buspower_dc` SMAJ36CA.
- Common-mode choke (100 µH) with 0 Ω bypass options, PESD2CANFD24V ESD protection, spark gaps.

## Differences between revisions

| Item                      | B125A                               | B125B                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| microSD card detect (PG4) | **Wrong** on A (`DET_B` → GND).     | **Fixed** on B (`DET_A` and `DET_B` both on `SDMMC_PRESENT`). Confirmed by the user. Not relevant for this crate. |
| PoE opto U12              | Symbol EL817 (value PS2911-1-F3-AX) | Symbol PS2911-1-F3-AX (same part, library change only)                                                            |

All MCU pin assignments, CAN channels, terminators and clocks are identical between the revisions.

## HIL notes

- The main board for **two-node bus tests on one MCU**: Ch1 (FDCAN1) ↔ Ch2 (FDCAN3) with a short DE-9 ↔ DE-9
  cable (CAN_H, CAN_L, GND). Terminators are switchable per channel, so error tests are possible too: no
  termination, ACK errors with one channel silent, and so on.
- Also the only board that tests FDCAN3 and two instances sharing the message RAM at runtime (FEATURES.md R4:
  zeroing must not wipe the other instance).
- Ethernet and USB HS are irrelevant to the driver, but could carry HIL results or drive an external reference
  node later.
- The OLED can show test status. The SPI1/UART7 test points can carry trigger lines to another
  board (e.g. for timestamp comparison with a B135).
