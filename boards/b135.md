### CAN bus side (isolated, `ISO_1` ground)

- DE-9 male J501: **pin 7 CAN_H, pin 2 CAN_L, pins 3 and 6 ISO GND, pin 5 shield, pin 9 bus V+**. Pins 1, 4 and 8 are not connected.
- CAN_H/CAN_L go through a 100 µH common-mode choke L501 (bypassed by 0 Ω R509/R510 options), with
  PESD2CANFD24V ESD protection and spark gaps.
- **Termination:** split 2 × 60 Ω with 4.7 nF to ISO GND, switched by the **latching** relay K501. It is
  powered from the isolated 5 V supply (MIE1W0505 DC/DC, always on).
  - A latching relay keeps its last state across resets and power cycles, so a HIL test must set it
    explicitly at start.
  - Pulse `TERM_EN` high for > 10 ms to connect the terminator, or `TERM_DIS` for > 10 ms to disconnect it. Keep
    both pins low otherwise and never drive both at once.
- **Bus power:** the board can be powered from DE-9 pin 9 (fuse, SMAJ58CA TVS, LT8304 isolated flyback →
  +5V0_SYS), as well as from USB-C.

## HIL notes

- One CAN channel only, so bus tests need a peer: a second B135, another test board, or a host USB-CAN adapter.
  Internal / external loopback tests need only the board and a probe.
- The switchable terminator allows tests of termination on/off, and makes error tests easy (no termination on a
  bus, or ACK errors with a single node).
- **B135B has a second CAN controller on J203: FDCAN3** (TX = pin 3 / PG9 AF2, RX = pin 4 / PD12 AF5, GND = pin 6),
  without a transceiver. Use it for single-wire CAN ([nucleo.md](nucleo.md#single-wire-can-without-transceivers)):
  FDCAN3 ↔ Nucleos / B129 FDCAN2. Or add an external transceiver and get FDCAN1 ↔ FDCAN3 on one board. The tied
  partner pins (PB3, PA7) must stay Hi-Z (input / analog) while FDCAN3 uses the header.
- The remaining J203 pins can carry trigger / sync lines between boards (e.g. for timestamp comparison).
- Board ID (RID dividers): ignore for now. Resistor values are unknown and may be wrong on some boards. Select the
  revision explicitly (cargo feature) instead.
